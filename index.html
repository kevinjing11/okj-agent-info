Private desktop agent

OKJ Intelligence

A private, local tool for the OKJ artist project. It helps its owner organize a music catalog, review a selected Gmail inbox, research public opportunities, and prepare private briefs and drafts.

How it works

OKJ Intelligence runs on the owner’s Mac. Its records, catalog, briefings, and connection settings are kept locally. It is not a public service and does not offer public accounts or sign-ups.

Optional connections

  • Gmail: the owner may authorize the dedicated OKJ mailbox with the Google gmail.readonly scope. The agent reads recent messages for local organization and briefing; it cannot send, edit, delete, label, or otherwise modify Gmail.
  • OpenAI: when the owner enables it, bounded content may be submitted to OpenAI to prepare private briefs or draft text. The agent does not provide the model with tools to send messages.
  • Tavily Search: when enabled by the owner, the agent searches public web results for music-relevant research leads. It does not sign in to social accounts or send direct messages.

Data and control

The agent keeps its persistent working records on the owner’s computer. Gmail authorization is optional and can be revoked in Google Account permissions. The owner can also disable the connection and manage local application state on the computer.

Read the Privacy Notice for the data practices and Google API Services disclosure.
